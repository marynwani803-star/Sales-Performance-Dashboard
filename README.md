                                                    Sales Performance Dashboard 

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand business performance, customer behavior and sales trends.

Tools: Excel (Power Pivot), SQL, Python (Pandas, Matplotlib), Power BI.

Excel
Imported and cleaned a subset of the data. Handled missing values in RegionName, IncomeLevel, ProductName, removed duplicate orders and fixed inconsistent category names. Documented all steps in Data Cleaning Log sheet. Built PivotTables and dashboard for Sales by Region, Product Category and Monthly Trends.

SQL
Imported cleaned data into SQL and linked Orders to Customers, Products and Regions with foreign keys. Queried for monthly revenue trends, top 10 customers by spending, regional performance, most profitable products and customer segments by income level.

Python
Used Jupyter Notebook. With Pandas I did EDA info, describe, isnull, grouped sales by region/category, calculated CLV, and created Profit Margin and Customer Segment. With Matplotlib I plotted monthly trend, region vs category heatmap, top 10 products, category share pie chart and spend by income.

Power BI
Built executive dashboard with KPIs (Total Revenue, Avg Order Value, Profit Margin, Customer Count), sales trend, category performance, regional map and customer segmentation.
